# Indexation audit, 2026-10-15

Owner ruling 2026-10-15: fix search reach. On 2026-10-12 the tracker scored #4
on search, absent from the top results for 5 of 5 queries. There is no Search
Console or Bing Webmaster access from a session, so this audit reads what a
crawler reads: paced single requests (one at a time, 4 to 6 seconds apart,
about 25 requests in total), browser and crawler user agents, bare URLs.

## Why we are still absent from results

Nothing above blocks indexing. The likely causes are age and authority, not a
defect: the city, company and role pages are days to weeks old, `/blog/` is a
subdirectory of a domain whose root is a separate app, and almost nothing links
in from outside. The levers left are the ones only the owner can pull (below)
plus inbound links, which the embeds and the citation file in the follow-up
PRs are for.

## What the owner does (about 15 minutes, once)

### Google Search Console

1. Open https://search.google.com/search-console and add a **Domain** property
   for `asktherecruiter.com` (DNS TXT record in Cloudflare; covers every
   subdirectory and the www variant).
2. Sitemaps: submit `https://asktherecruiter.com/blog/sitemap_index.xml`. Then
   submit the three tracker sitemaps on their own so each gets its own
   indexed-count line:
   - `https://asktherecruiter.com/blog/talent-intelligence-tracker/company-sitemap.xml`
   - `https://asktherecruiter.com/blog/talent-intelligence-tracker/places-sitemap.xml`
   - `https://asktherecruiter.com/blog/talent-intelligence-tracker/roles-sitemap.xml`
3. URL Inspection, then **Request indexing**, for these five (the daily quota is
   about ten):
   - `/blog/talent-intelligence-tracker/`
   - `/blog/talent-intelligence-tracker/places/`
   - `/blog/talent-intelligence-tracker/city/london/`
   - `/blog/talent-intelligence-tracker/city/new-york/`
   - `/blog/talent-intelligence-tracker/company/openai/`
4. In URL Inspection, run **Test live URL** on one city page and confirm
   "Page can be indexed" and that the rendered screenshot shows the employer
   list. That is the verified-Googlebot check a session cannot do.
5. After 7 days, read **Pages > Why pages aren't indexed** and send the reasons
   with counts. "Discovered, currently not indexed" means authority; "Crawled,
   currently not indexed" means quality; anything else is a defect for us.
6. Enhancements: confirm Dataset, Breadcrumb and FAQ reports show no errors.

### Bing Webmaster Tools

1. Open https://www.bing.com/webmasters and choose **Import from Google Search
   Console** (one click once step 1 above is done), or add the site and verify
   by the same DNS record.
2. Submit the same four sitemaps.
3. **IndexNow** report: within a day of the next collect run it should show
   submissions from `/blog/`. If it shows none, tell a session; the key is the
   sibling's and its health is visible there.
